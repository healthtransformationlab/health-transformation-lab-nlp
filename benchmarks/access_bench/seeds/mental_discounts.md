# Seeds: mental health discounts and vouchers (planned, spec 044 US3)

Personal learning project, not affiliated. Seed ideas for AccessBench's **mental** section, which is planned, not
scored ([ADR 0014](../../../docs/adr/0014-accessbench-oral-medical-mental-sections.md)). Each source was opened on
2026-11-25. These become scored items only after the oral section's review process has run once
([docs/access_review_protocol.md](../../../docs/access_review_protocol.md)).

1. Someone in distress who can't pay for therapy: the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline lets them call, text or chat, free and confidential, 24/7/365. Source: https://988lifeline.org/
2. Finding low-cost treatment for depression or substance use: FindTreatment.gov ("SAMHSA's confidential and anonymous resource" for mental and substance use disorder treatment) and SAMHSA's National Helpline, 1-800-662-HELP (4357). Sources: https://findtreatment.gov/ (title and description), https://www.cdc.gov/mental-health/caring/index.html (helpline number)
3. A parent of an uninsured child who needs mental health care: Medicaid/CHIP/Marketplace enrollment, and low-cost care at a community health center. Source: https://www.cdc.gov/mental-health/caring/index.html ("Mental Health Resources | Mental Health | CDC", May 12, 2026)

Before any mental-health item is scored, the section needs a crisis-safety rule: an answer to someone in danger must
put immediate help (988, emergency services) first, whatever else it scores (ADR 0014).

Not yet sourced: certified community behavioral health clinics (samhsa.gov refused the request), sliding-scale
therapy rules.
