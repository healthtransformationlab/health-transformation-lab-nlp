# Seeds: medical discounts and vouchers (planned, spec 044 US3)

Personal learning project, not affiliated. Seed ideas for AccessBench's **medical** section, which is planned, not
scored ([ADR 0014](../../../docs/adr/0014-accessbench-oral-medical-mental-sections.md)). Each source was opened on
2026-11-25. These become scored items only after the oral section's review process has run once
([docs/access_review_protocol.md](../../../docs/access_review_protocol.md)).

1. A patient with an unaffordable bill from a nonprofit hospital: ask for the hospital's financial assistance policy, its application and plain language summary; someone found eligible can't be charged more than amounts generally billed. Source: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/financial-assistance-policy-and-emergency-medical-care-policy-section-501r4
2. A hospital bill that is already in collections: under the nonprofit hospital rules there is a 120-day notification period and a 240-day application period, counted from the first post-discharge bill, before extraordinary collection actions such as credit reporting. Source: https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/billing-and-collections-section-501r6
3. An ER or lab bill a provider offers to put on a medical credit card: deferred interest, high APRs, and asking about financial assistance or a zero-interest plan first. Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/cfpb-report-highlights-costly-credit-cards-and-loans-pushed-on-patients/
