The two moves

Make the test fair, and show the gain

1 · The main move

Put the right questions on the test

Write the questions the tests leave out: where medical meets dental (medicines, chronic disease, emergencies), and the social factors of care (cost, coverage, distance, transportation). Each one traces back to published evidence.

2 · Then show the lift

Show what the evidence adds

Publish one open AI agent built on public research and guidelines, to show and encourage measurable lift in the leading AI models on those questions.

What gets tested is what AI developers improve. A high score helps one model. A fairer test helps everyone.

Health Transformation Lab · Drill-down

Two things. First, we put the missing questions on the benchmarks: where medical meets dental, and the social factors of care. We write them, and we take them to the people who run the tests. Second, we show what the evidence adds: one open AI agent built on public research and guidelines, connected to leading AI models, with the gain measured in public. Why the first one matters most: what gets tested is what AI developers improve. A high score helps one model. A fairer test helps everyone. If asked how the agent works: someone asks a question in the AI tool they already use, the AI checks the published evidence through the agent's connection, and answers from it with the source shown; see the next slide. If asked whether the benchmark owners will agree: it is their decision, so we ask as partners; the dental test's authors note it covers clinical dentistry only and that their expert panel came from one institution, so there is an opening.
