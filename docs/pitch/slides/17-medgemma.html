The open option: Google’s MedGemma

A model we could own and publish

What it is

Google’s open AI models for medical text and medical images. Free to download and adapt.

Why it fits

It reads medical images, and Google’s list does not include dental. A dental version would be new, and open to share.

What it takes

De-identified data, a privacy and legal review, and modest computing.

Today most health AI is rented. This would be an open model anyone can check. Google says MedGemma is not yet clinical-grade, so this supports research and tools, not clinical decisions.

Source: Google Health AI Developer Foundations, MedGemma (page updated Jan 13, 2026) · Drill-down

Use this if someone asks whether there could be an open oral health model. A project this size doesn't build a frontier model. But Google publishes MedGemma, a family of open medical AI models that anyone can download and adapt. It handles medical text and images such as CT, MRI and pathology slides. Dental imaging is not on Google's list, so a version tuned on dental data would be a real contribution. It would come after the 180-day first step, and only with de-identified or synthetic data and a privacy and legal review, because a tuned model can memorize what it was trained on. Google states MedGemma is not yet clinical-grade and needs validation for each use. Check Google's current terms of use before publishing a tuned version.
