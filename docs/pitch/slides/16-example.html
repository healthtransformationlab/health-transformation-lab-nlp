Picture an emergency room at 2 a.m. A patient comes in with a swollen jaw. The doctor asks an AI tool what to do. That AI was tested on medicine, not on teeth. On a dental test, twelve leading AI models averaged 22 percent on case questions. This is where medical and dental meet now. Dental, medical and emergency records, linked in one shared format such as OMOP, could make that answer better, and the same linked records are what a fair test of it is built from. If asked: the scenario is an illustration; the 22 percent is from GlobalDentBench, a public dental test with questions from 88 countries.
