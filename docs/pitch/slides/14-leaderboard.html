Context matters. Relationships matter.

Today oral health plays the AI game in private, and unmeasured. Time to rethink publishing: papers, plus an AI agent that is tested in public.

Source: HealthBench leaderboard, benchmarklist.com, updated Sep 29, 2026 · Health Transformation Lab · Drill-down

How do you pick a hospital or a college? Most of us check the rankings. AI gets picked the same way. These are the top-ranked AI models for health. We are not competing with them. They are the teams, and oral health evidence is a talented rookie no scout has seen. So we publish one open AI agent built on public evidence, connect it to these models, and measure how much better each one gets at oral health. That gain is the uplift, and we publish it. Context matters, and so do relationships: between the mouth and the rest of the body, and between the people who treat each. To say correctly if a technical person asks: the ranking shown is for general health questions, and it lists AI models only. The uplift is measured on oral health questions, with each model on its own and then with the agent. The first test may use three leading models from different developers, not necessarily these three. An agent is a model connected to tools and data. The published connection is called an MCP server, MCP being the open standard that lets these models use it. We do not build or own the models.
