Illustrative question. Clinicians and scientists choose the real questions and the evidence behind each · Drill-down

This is what one benchmark question looks like. On the test is the question, the kind a person without a dentist actually asks. Behind it is the right answer, written by clinicians and scientists: what a correct, safe answer has to include, and what would make it wrong. And one click further are the published studies and guidelines the question and answer come from. So when a leading AI model takes the test, it is measured against the same science, and anyone who doubts the answer can drill down to the source. A medical-dental question works the same way: a person with diabetes asks why their gums bleed, and the right answer covers both the dentist and the doctor. The questions on these slides are illustrations; the real questions and the evidence behind each are chosen by the people who write the test.
