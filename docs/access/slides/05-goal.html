The goal

Better answers where people already ask

1. Measure

Grade the AI people use

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others, on the same questions and the same grading, with each model's version and date recorded.

2. Share

Show each company what its AI missed

The questions, the answer keys and the steps each model left out, so the companies behind these tools can fix them.

3. Re-measure

Track progress every round

Run the same questions again and report, model by model, whether answers give more of the steps people need, with less harm.

Success is measured, not claimed: the share of needed steps each model gives, round after round. Better answers in the tools millions already use reach more people than any one clinic or website can.

Personal project, not affiliated with any organization · AccessBench: Oral Health · 5

This is what AccessBench is for. People already get their health information from a handful of AI tools, so the fastest way to improve what they hear is to improve those tools. The loop has three steps. Measure: grade the AI people actually use, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others, on the same questions with the same grading, recording each model's version and the date. Share: publish what each model missed and give the companies behind these tools the questions, the answer keys and the failures, so they can fix them. Re-measure: run the same questions again every round and report, model by model, whether answers give more of the steps people need, with less harm. Success is measured, not claimed: the share of needed steps each model gives, round after round. Better answers in tools that millions already use reach more people than any one clinic or website can.
