People are not waiting for AI to be approved for health questions. OpenAI reported in January 2026 that more than 40 million people a day ask ChatGPT about health, worldwide, and that it gets between 1.6 and 1.9 million health-insurance messages a week. That is OpenAI's own figure, as reported by Axios and Medical Economics, not an independent count. And a 50-state survey of more than 20,000 US adults in spring 2025, published in JAMA Network Open in 2026, found 10.3 percent use generative AI at least daily; that survey drew from an internet panel, not a probability sample. The point is the kind of question: coverage, bills, appeals and where to go. Those used to go to a benefits counselor, a front desk or a legal-aid line.
