Run 608662392694095, synthetic questions, preliminary (one AI judge) · AccessBench: Oral Health · 6

This is the first run on the revised questions, on four free, open AI models hosted on Databricks: OpenAI's gpt-oss-120b, Google's Gemma 3 12B and two of Meta's Llama models. Each bar answers one question: of the steps a person needed to get care, what share did the AI give? gpt-oss-120b gave 77.7 percent and Gemma 72.7 percent; the two Llama models gave about a third, 32.5 and 29.1 percent. Under each bar: how many answers gave a clear next step, meaning what to do first, whom to call, when, and a fallback; and how many answers could lead to harm, such as a delay in care or an avoidable bill, or seriously, such as skipping needed care or a large cost. Overall the answers gave 187 of the 352 steps people needed, about half. One caution: every answer was capped at 1,500 tokens, about 1,100 words, and gpt-oss-120b hit that limit on 18 of 22 questions, so its answers were cut off and part of its score may come from listing everything. The grades come from one AI judge not yet checked by an independent person, so they are preliminary. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are not in this run yet.
