AccessBench · exploratory · November 2026

Millions ask AI how to get care.

Does the answer get them there?

AccessBench grades whether an AI answer helps someone actually get care: is it covered, what to do about a denial or a bill, where to go that they can afford.

The third benchmark, after PerioBench (finding gum disease in dental records) and OralHealthBench (accurate answers where medicine meets dentistry).

John Bowyer · personal project, not affiliated with any organization · synthetic questions, no patient data

People now ask AI the questions they used to bring to a benefits counselor or a dental office's front desk: is this covered, what do I do about this bill, where can I go that I can afford. AccessBench grades whether the answer actually gets them to care. It is the third of three benchmarks: PerioBench asks whether we can find gum disease in the dental record, and OralHealthBench asks whether AI gives accurate answers where medicine meets dentistry. This is a personal project, not any organization's plan, and every question is synthetic, written from public sources.
