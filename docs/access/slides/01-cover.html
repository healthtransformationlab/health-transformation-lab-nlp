AccessBench: Oral Health · exploratory · November 2026

Millions ask AI how to get care.

Does the answer get them there?

AccessBench grades whether the AI people already use helps them actually get care, shares what each model misses with the companies behind it, and tracks whether the answers improve, round after round.

Oral health first. Coming soon: AccessBench: Medical Care and AccessBench: Mental Health.

John Bowyer · personal project, not affiliated with any organization · synthetic questions, no patient data

People now ask AI the questions they used to bring to a benefits counselor or a dental office's front desk: is this covered, what do I do about this bill, where can I go that I can afford. AccessBench grades whether the answer actually gets them to care, shows the companies behind these tools what their AI missed, and re-measures every round so progress can be tracked. This deck is AccessBench: Oral Health, about dental care; AccessBench: Medical Care and AccessBench: Mental Health are coming soon, built the same way. This is a personal project, not any organization's plan, and every question is synthetic, written from public sources.
