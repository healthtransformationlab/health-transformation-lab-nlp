Coverage

Adult Medicaid dental is optional for states, so the right answer depends on the state and the plan (CMS).

Denials

They said no. What now?

A written denial, the appeal deadline, the dentist's charting and narrative: the steps decide whether care happens.

The visit itself

Did I get what I needed?

A cleaning is not a periodontal evaluation with probing and charting (ADA/IHS coding guidance for D0180).

Most health AI benchmarks ask whether the facts are right. For getting care, the facts are only the start. Three things decide whether someone gets a gum check. Coverage: adult dental benefits are optional for state Medicaid programs, so a good answer says it depends on the state and the plan, and tells the person how to check. Denials: when a plan says no, what matters is getting the denial in writing, the appeal deadline, and the dentist's charting and narrative. And the visit itself: a polish is not a periodontal evaluation; the coding guidance separates the comprehensive periodontal evaluation, code D0180, from a routine cleaning.
