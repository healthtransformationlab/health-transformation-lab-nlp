These are the first ten scored questions, all about one thing: getting a periodontal screening. Each began as a real question people asked in public, on forums, or as a gap named in a KFF survey or Indian Health Service coding guidance, and was rewritten as a synthetic ask with no one's own words or details. They cover Medicaid coverage that differs by state, including pregnancy; denials and appeals; waiting periods on private plans; the Medicare dental exclusion and its narrow exceptions, and the common belief that diabetes moves gum care onto medical insurance; and whether the visit that was booked is the evaluation the person needs. Every rubric cites published statutes, CMS pages or guidelines.
