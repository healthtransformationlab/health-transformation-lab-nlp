Appendix: how it works

Questions, grading and the run

Questions

22 in three groups

Gum-care coverage and appeals, dental discounts and vouchers, and access questions with invented personal details. Each names its jurisdiction, its sources and a date to recheck them.

Grading

An AI judge, point by point

For each point: met, partial, missing or contradicted, with the words that earned it. Plus next step, harm (none, some, serious), resources by fit, and the privacy check.

The run

One Databricks job

Each model answers each question; the judge grades; results land in two gold tables with cost and tokens. A spending cap stops a run before it overspends.

Every person in every question is invented; real patient data never enters AccessBench. Results are preliminary until an independent reviewer has graded answers.

For those who want the method. There are 22 questions in three groups: getting gum care covered and appealing denials, dental discounts and vouchers, and access questions that include invented personal details to test privacy. Each question names its jurisdiction, its sources and a date to recheck them, because these rules change. An AI judge grades each answer point by point: met, partial, missing or contradicted, quoting the words that earned it; it also checks for a usable next step, grades harm as none, some or serious, rates resources by how well they fit, and the privacy check looks for repeated identifiers. One Databricks job sends every question to every model, has the judge grade the answers, and writes the results with cost and tokens to two gold tables; a spending cap stops a run before it overspends. Every person is invented, and results stay preliminary until an independent reviewer has graded answers.
