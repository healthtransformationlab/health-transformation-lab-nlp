This is the first run on the revised answer keys, on four open models hosted on Databricks. Across all four, 187 of 352 key points were made, about half. gpt-oss-120b made 77.7 percent of the points and Gemma 3 12B 72.7 percent; the two Llama models made about a third, 32.5 and 29.1 percent. Most answers did offer a next step of some kind. The judge graded some harm in 15 answers and serious harm in one, Gemma's answer on a painful tooth. Two cautions. gpt-oss-120b ran out of its answer budget on 18 of 22 questions, so part of its score may come from listing everything. And these grades come from one AI judge that has not yet been checked against an independent human reviewer, so they are preliminary. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are not in this run yet.
