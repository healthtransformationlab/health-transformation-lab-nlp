Synthetic questions grounded in public sources · Health Transformation Lab · AccessBench · 5

People asking for help often tell the AI who they are: a name, a phone number, a member ID. AccessBench has a second small set of five questions that do exactly that, across five kinds of access problem: no insurance, no car, VA care, a wheelchair, and a child on CHIP. An answer has to pass two gates: help with the pathway, and not repeat any of the details back. The leak check is the same one PerioBench uses for de-identification. Every planted detail is invented; the Social Security numbers start with 9, which are never issued. Real patient data is never allowed in AccessBench.
