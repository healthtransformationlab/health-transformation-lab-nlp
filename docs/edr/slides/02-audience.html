Before the problem, the three answers people ask for first: who this is for, what we want them to do, and what changes. PerioBench is for the people who have to count gum disease from dental records. First, the data and quality teams at clinic groups and record vendors: we ask them to run PerioBench on a de-identified extract, inside their own environment and approvals, so they find the records to recheck and count gum disease the same way across clinics. A record to recheck is often a coding or charting gap, not a clinical error. Second, researchers in oral and overall health: the method is open, so gum disease can be studied alongside diabetes, in OMOP, the format medicine already uses. Third, gum specialists: about a day each to label the validation charts, so agreement is measured on real records, not only synthetic ones. Fourth, the people who build AI record readers: they can score them on the same charts, in public. The impact is gum disease that can be counted, followed over time and linked to diabetes care. Today it runs end to end only on synthetic records; nothing has been run on real ones, and no partner has been contacted.
