Synthetic charts, scored against the generator's truth: not clinical performance · real model runs, CPU, 2026-10-26 · 4

These are real model runs, on synthetic charts, scored against the generator's truth; they are not clinical performance, and agreement with simulated reviewers is a dry run we do not show. Four numbers out of 240 review charts. Rules that combine the whole record, reading the gum measurements first, got 234 right. Reading only the free-text note, a small model trained on dental notes, ModernBERT with a keyword rule for the most severe stage, got 219. A bigger medical AI, MedGemma 1.5 4B, used as is without training and with the same stage 4 rule, got 185, about the same as simple keyword rules at 189; alone it gets about 182. And any single piece of evidence falls short: the gum measurements alone got 193 right, because 45 of the 240 charts had no measurements at all. Two lessons: combine the evidence, and train on the domain. If asked about uncertainty: every number has a 95 percent bootstrap interval on the full results page; on 240 charts, many methods cannot be told apart. If asked whether MedGemma can improve: few-shot and fine-tuned runs are next, on a GPU. If asked why synthetic: no identifiable patient data may be used here; synthetic data lets us build and test everything, including the privacy checks, before any approvals.
