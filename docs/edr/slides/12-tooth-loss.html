The full tooth-loss scores behind the cost slide, all on synthetic records. On extractions whose reason was recorded and held out of training, 469 teeth, the models find about three in four of the teeth lost to gum disease and rule out about nine in ten of the others, with an AUC of about 0.9. Two label sets are shown: completed extractions only, and completed plus planned extractions whose tooth is later recorded missing. The bottom row is the one to watch: on the 10,761 teeth already missing at the first visit, which is where the model is used, the AUC is 0.59 to 0.70, and 0.59 to 0.77 across five generator seeds.
