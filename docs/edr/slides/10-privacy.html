Synthetic notes with fake names and dates; also tested on public benchmark sets · Health Transformation Lab · the dental record (EDR) · 10

Before any software or person reads a note, names, dates and IDs come out. We compared 15 methods on 400 synthetic notes with fake identifiers planted in them. The best was a combination of tools with a guard that protects dental words; it still missed 115 of 2,714 identifiers, which is why a final check holds back any note with one left, and nothing goes on until it passes. It also removed 41 of 2,462 dental terms that look like names or codes, such as Glickman or Gracey 11/12, which would damage what the AI reads. The two numbers pull against each other, like a spam filter. We also tested on public benchmark sets, including published, already de-identified case reports. All synthetic, or published by others already de-identified.
