Synthetic charts, scored against the generator's truth at five status groups (healthy and gingivitis together), adults from 2021-04-01: the 223 review charts · real model runs on CPU · 5

These are real model runs, on synthetic charts, scored against the generator's truth; they are not clinical performance, and agreement with simulated reviewers is a dry run we do not show. Numbers out of 223 review charts: the notes were rebuilt for adults' visits from April 2021, the start date the scores use, with healthy and gingivitis counted as one group. Rules that combine the whole record, reading the gum measurements first, got 207 right. Reading only the free-text note, a simple word-count model with a keyword rule for the most severe stage got 191, and the keyword rules alone 183. The trained encoders such as ModernBERT and the medical AI MedGemma read the notes before the rebuild, so their runs are kept but not scored here: they are being rerun on the new notes. And any single piece of evidence falls short: the gum measurements alone got 166 right, because 53 of the 223 charts had no measurements at all. The lesson: combine the evidence. If asked about uncertainty: every number has a 95 percent bootstrap interval on the full results page; on 223 charts, many methods cannot be told apart. If asked about MedGemma and the trained encoders: their reruns on the new notes are on a GPU, then few-shot and fine-tuned runs. If asked why synthetic: no identifiable patient data may be used here; synthetic data lets us build and test everything, including the privacy checks, before any approvals.
