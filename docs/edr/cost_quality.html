PerioBench: cost beside quality, three questions

Back to the deck
A personal learning project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any organization. Synthetic data only: scores are against the generator's answer key, not clinical performance. Costs use dated list prices and illustrative hardware prices.

Each question has its own measure and its own pass bar, so each gets its own chart: cost is never compared across them. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a question's title to drill in, and click a method for its score, 95% range, cost and sources. A method that was not run shows its price only, never a score.
