# Disclaimer

**This is a personal learning project.** I built it on my own time to learn
and practice synthetic data generation, OMOP modeling, NLP evaluation and
Databricks, while waiting for any internal approvals that real work in this
area would need.

- **Not affiliated.** This repository is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
  produced for any employer, institution, company or research group, or any
  vendor mentioned in the docs.
- **Not official business or research.** Nothing here reflects the plans,
  methods, data, findings, priorities or positions of any organization. It is
  not a study protocol, not a research deliverable, and not a commitment to
  build anything.
- **Synthetic patients.** The study's patient records are synthetic. No
  identifiable patient data (PHI), internal data or internal documents are
  used. To test the de-identification step, tools are also scored on clinical
  text that others have published already de-identified: MACCROBAT case
  reports (CC BY 4.0), kept in `data/external/` with their source and licence,
  and John Snow Labs' 48-note benchmark, which stays outside this repository
  under its own terms.
- **Public information only.** Organizations and products are mentioned only
  as described in their own public sources, which are cited.
- **No results.** Numbers in the docs come from simulated data, simulated
  reviewers and illustrative prices. They are exercises, not findings, and
  must not be cited as evidence about any product, method or population.
  Scores measured on public datasets (MACCROBAT) are real measurements of
  this repo's code on that dataset only, with small samples; the same
  caution applies.
