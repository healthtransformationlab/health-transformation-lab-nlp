# PR Review: patients with no natural teeth in the Open Dental extract, and optional states

## Summary
The synthetic Open Dental extract now has patients with no natural teeth, drawn by age band at published US rates
on a random stream of their own. They have 32 teeth missing at entry and no perio chart, radiograph, hygiene visit,
extraction or answer-key rows, and the tooth-loss model, the stage recount and the charting rates leave them out. A
new check (`chart_without_teeth`) flags a chart recorded for one. An optional setting, off by default, gives each
clinic a US state and scales the share by that state's BRFSS 2022 rate. The tooth-loss results, the presets, the
data-quality page, the silver dictionary and the committed tooth table were rerun with the repo's scripts, and the
data card moves edentulism from "Known gaps" to sourced parameters.

## Scans
- **Secrets:** none in the diff, data files included.
- **PHI / real data:** none. The only real data is aggregate public NHANES rates by age band (this repo's own run of
  public files) and CDC state rates. No row-level records.
- **Organization claims:** none. No organisation, clinic or person is named beyond cited publications and a public
  CDC dataset.

## Sources checked
- **NCHS Data Brief 368 (item 79):** 8.9% at 65-69, 10.6% at 70-74, 17.8% at 75+, 12.9% at 65+. These match
  `Params.edentulous_share_by_age` and the card.
- **This repo's NHANES 2009-2014 run** (`nhanes_edentulous_by_age.csv`, written by `scripts/38_nhanes_perio.py`):
  0.8%, 1.3%, 3.7% and 7.9% below 65. These match the setting, rounded.
- **CDC U.S. Chronic Disease Indicators ORH03, BRFSS 2022 (item 81):** re-fetched from data.cdc.gov during this
  review. All 51 state values and the US value equal `STATE_EDENTULOUS_65_PCT`.
- **America's Health Rankings:** could not be opened, so it isn't cited, as the card says.

## Findings
- 🔵 **Nit:** in the geography option, the state's 65+ ratio is applied at every age. This is a documented placeholder
  assumption, and no committed result uses states.
- 🔵 **Nit:** the under-65 rates are from NHANES 2009-2014, and complete tooth loss is falling (item 80), so they may
  run a little high. The card and the code say so.
- 🔵 **Nit:** the seed-42 held-out AUC under the defaults falls from 0.87 to 0.72. The set is small (89 teeth), and the
  5-seed means move about 0.04, inside the seed spread. `docs/tooth_loss.md` explains this rather than hiding it.
- 🔵 **Note:** open PR #513 (029 US9) also edits `docs/tooth_loss.md` and the tooth-loss results. Whichever merges
  second must rerun `scripts/51_tooth_loss_model.py` and the presets, not merge the numbers by hand.
